## Namespace and the nonsense-path control — **this site is a catch-all**

`https://thebase.com/zz-this-does-not-exist` returns **HTTP 200 with the homepage**: the final
URL rewrites to `https://thebase.com/`, and the title, the 1,435-character body and all four
controls are identical to the homepage reading.

Recorded because it constrains what this reference may claim. **No sub-route of `thebase.com`
can be cited as evidence of anything**, because every path serves the same document. The
homepage reading itself stands — it was reached at the canonical URL and is what the browser
renders there — but nothing here rests on a second route of this host, and the second source is
a different host entirely.

(The same control disqualified `hisense.com` from the CN sweep on the same day. The difference
is that BASE has a real second brand-owned surface and hisense did not.)

All 80 custom properties carry BASE's own naming. No framework prefix (`--tw-`, `--wp-`,
`--el-`, `--bs-`) appears at the document element.

### Motion measured on a control, not just declared

Both pink CTAs compute `transition: color 0.2s cubic-bezier(0.3, 0.7, 0.7, 0.3)`. That is
`--duration-hover` `.2s` and `--ease-link` `cubic-bezier(.3, .7, .7, .3)`, read off a rendered
element rather than off the stylesheet — two independent readings of one motion contract.

It also explains why the pink buttons look inert: the declared transition animates **`color`**,
and on a solid pink button with white text neither value moves.

### Focus is Chrome's

All three declared controls match `:focus-visible` and compute
`outline: rgb(0, 95, 204) auto 1px`, with `outline-style` moving `none` → `auto`. That is the
browser's default ring, established against a control page carrying no author stylesheet
(`docs/MEASUREMENT_METHOD_2026-09-22.md` §2.5). **No focus token is recorded.**

## Second surface — binc.jp

`https://binc.jp/` (BASE, Inc.'s corporate site; the request settles on `/about/history`,

It renders **`TazuganeGothicStdN-Medium` and `-Light`** on 53 text elements — confirming that
the face declared in `--font-family-base` is one BASE actually ships, even though the marketing
surface sets Gilroy over it. Cited for ownership and for the typeface; not for tokens.

## Evidence limitations

- **`thebase.com` is a catch-all**, so no route of that host other than the one measured can be
  cited, and none is.
- **Three controls**, all on the marketing surface. The shop dashboard was not opened, so the
  `--ease-drawer`, `--ease-progressbar` and `--ease-chart-pie*` curves are recorded from the
  token set and **were not observed animating anything**.
- **No focus token, no shadow token, no disabled state** — none exists or none was observed.
- The fourth pink CTA is excluded because its hover could not be measured.
- The `bbq` colour group is described here and not promoted; nothing on the page paints it.
- Desktop 1440×1000, light scheme, logged out.
