### Two collectors

A delegated subagent (Sonnet) ran the deep probe. Re-measured independently: the headline claim —
Ocado's `--salt-*` is not J.P. Morgan's Salt — by diffing names against the published
`@salt-ds/theme@1.46.0/css/theme.css` (1,869 `--salt-*` names): **0 of Ocado's 156 match**. Also
re-measured: Log in (hover `#c9bcd3`), the Register card (hover shadow
`rgba(0,0,0,0.08) 0 2px 12px`), Add on `/search?entry=bread` (hover `#e6a300`); all matched, and
all three show Chrome's `auto` ring on focus. The purple primary button (the Live Chat launcher)
is the subagent's reading, consistent with `--salt-button-primary-hover-background-color`.

The subagent also traced every `--salt-*` and `--intent-*` declaration to first-party stylesheets
on `ocado.com` (`theme-variables.css`, `static/style-*.css`) via `sheet.href`. Its note that 186
elements compute the UA link blue `#0000ee` was made without a visibility check and is not used.

## The `--salt-*` question

Beyond the name diff: J.P. Morgan Salt is built of semantic, camelCase-suffixed tokens over a
`--salt-color-*` / `--salt-palette-*` primitive layer (e.g.
`--salt-actionable-accented-bold-borderColor-hover`); Ocado's are component-named, kebab-case, with
no primitive layer (`--salt-button-primary-hover-background-color`, `--salt-links-bar-*`,
`--salt-elevation-shadow-*`). Salt's spacing runs `--salt-spacing-50…900`; Ocado's doubles
(`0, 05, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32`). Ocado's brand hexes (`4d216d`, `f1c500`, `c51d25`) do not occur in
Salt's theme. Recorded as Ocado's own system under a coincident prefix.
