## Doubts and boundaries

- The root nonsense path is a 200 navigation catch-all; it supplies no 404 or design-system proof. Real brand/company pages have separately opened, differentiated bodies and titles.
- No official design-system documentation, standalone brand manual, product-font statement or font licence was verified. Targeted searches included `site:netbk.co.jp "デザインシステム" OR "ブランドガイドライン" OR "フォント"`; absence from results is not proof of nonexistence.
- The public consumer homepage is measured, not the authenticated bank, mobile app, BaaS partner edition or corporate service. Those surfaces receive no transferred component or font claims.
- Body/action-link computed family begins with `hiragino sans`, which is not listed as a loaded face. Its actual platform glyph provider is unresolved; no substitute was chosen and no unresolved family is promoted.
- The census includes other loaded aliases without proving their role throughout the page. Asset URLs are @font-face declarations; the selected network font format was not captured and licensing was not checked.
- Search has only unchanged or browser-auto states and is excluded from components. The image banner's computed anchor underline is recorded, but a visible image-pixel response was not verified; its lazy-loaded height is not focus styling and the banner remains prose-only.
- All pointer presses were made while hovered; equality to hover is an observation, not evidence for a separate active-only selector. No disabled, validation, success or error states were induced.
- All five links and the input use browser `outline-style:auto` in this capture. No authored focus token was found on these controls; this is not a site-wide absence claim.
- No rejection was logged. Screenshots show no consent banner on the measured homepage, so no reject-button label could be extracted there. The corporate HTML's “×”/“OK” cookie notice is reported separately.
- The census is an element/style histogram, not a pixel-area estimate. Some low-frequency colours may be transient loading content. They are not promoted merely because they appear in the census.
- The probe compares computed styles/attributes, not image pixels, canvas/video output, closed shadow roots, cross-origin iframe contents, external SVG internals or arbitrary siblings. Only desktop was rendered; mobile CSS rules are labelled as declarations.
- Assembler claims are generated against the root homepage automatically; the measured final `/contents/` path and CSS source provenance are retained here rather than modifying generated verification metadata.
