that most of them are not on the brand's surface:

| observation | date | found |

A homepage is one surface and a palette covers several, so a figure under 100% is
expected and is not by itself evidence of anything. What carries weight here is the

and it did not. `tokens.source` is `prose-derived` — these values were transcribed from
documentation rather than measured, and the measurement does not support them.

components and prose that reuse it — in the nineteen worst references every declared
colour is reused elsewhere in the document, without exception — so this is authoring
work, not a token edit. It is recorded rather than silently fixed.
