## Doubts and deliberately absent fields

- **All pressed states are UNMEASURED**, not unchanged and not disabled. The raw reason is `:active did not match while the mouse button was down`; no pressed token is emitted.
- **Hover crops fail validation.** Valid computed hover snapshots precede `:hover stopped matching during the crop` on all six controls. Report hover as raw matched-state observations, and let the orchestrator re-measure. The cause of crop/pressed loss was not established; no unsupported overlay explanation is asserted.
- **Navigation focus palette is polluted.** Six recorded colour/inheritance changes persist after blur. Only the outline in `changedVsAfterBlur` is harvested as focus; offset -2px is preserved.
- **Text font remains unresolved.** Only icon fonts are loaded in the census. The distributed Roboto-font assets and Japanese stacks receive metadata but no specimen, no typography family token and no replacement. Substitute: none chosen.
- **Search is intentionally absent from components.** Hover/focus were measured but unchanged within scope, and press was not measured. Its geometry and type metrics remain in prose.
- **Body ink is mixed.** Dominant paragraph ink is navy, much of it inside topic links. The neutral alpha ink remains in prose and is not flattened into a guessed hex.
- **Namespace exclusions are conservative.** Ownership of 35 widget-style declarations is unresolved; they are excluded. Nine cross-origin sheets are unreadable in the CSSOM walk. Two linked CSS files were opened directly to establish first-party provenance, not to pretend the declaration census was complete.
- **Current design narrative is a dated first-party timeline.** Its disclaimer says November 2025 even though Phase 9 lists February 2026. App rollout and authenticated trading were not independently exercised.
- **Parent evidence is historical.** The 2008 entry names SBIホールディングス㈱; no current percentage was verified, and neither legal notice names a parent.
- Mobile breakpoints, mobile layouts, touch states, authenticated workflows, semantic feedback states, disabled behaviour, animation timing, reduced motion, font licences and a formal persona set were not measured or verified.
