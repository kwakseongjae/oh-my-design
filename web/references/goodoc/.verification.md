### Comparison boundary and visual checks

The raw `compared` string for each control is copied below. All compare self and its ::before/::after, descendants and their pseudo-elements, and three ancestor levels with pseudo-elements. Descendant counts are 0 for app-download/login/search, 5 for hospital-nav (one image), 6 for vaccine-card (one SVG and one path), and 6 for open-now (one image). Each no-change statement is limited to that computed-style/DOM scope. It does not cover pixels, external sprite contents, canvas/video, siblings, closed shadow roots or cross-origin iframes.

The viewport screenshot was inspected after the survey and pass 1. The primary button's hover and pressed crops and the search keyboard-focus crop in pass 2 were inspected and match their intended controls. The app-download pointer crops have no shotError. Other pointer-state snapshots match hover/active, but the crop validation subsequently reports `:hover stopped matching during the crop` / `:active stopped matching during the crop`; those PNGs do not independently prove the sampled states. These raw computed samples are retained with that limitation. The card-size observations are not accepted as brand behaviour.

All focus `persistsAfterBlur` lists are empty. Search's immediate ancestor returns to `rgb(246, 247, 249)` and a transparent 1px border after blur, so its white/dark-border treatment is a focus-specific change rather than a persistent polluted baseline. In contrast, no authored focus colour is derived from any `auto` outline. Disabled status was false at control identity and at keyboard landing; no disabled appearance was measured.
