# Nulab — Verification Notes (2026-09-22)

Pipeline: JP expansion wave 1, brand #1 · Brand: Nulab / 株式会社ヌーラボ (Fukuoka)

## Scope and collector boundary

- Playwright-driven Chrome, 1440×1000, `ja-JP` with a matching `accept-language`, light scheme
  forced, `getComputedStyle` on the document element and on individual controls, clicks
  suppressed in the capture phase, each page scrolled once and returned to the top.
- Logged out. No account was created and no sign-in was attempted.
- Desktop only. The products themselves (Backlog, Cacoo, Nulab Pass, Flowbase) were not opened.

## Namespace ownership and the nonsense-path control

`https://nulab.com/ja/zz-this-does-not-exist` returns a real **HTTP 404** page titled
`404: Not found`, with its own content and `lang="en"` — a different route, not the homepage.
The site is not a catch-all.

**60 of the 206 custom properties are WordPress**, not Nulab: `--wp--preset--font-size--*`,
`--wp--preset--color--*`, `--wp-admin-theme-color-*`, `--wp-bound-block-color`. They are
excluded by prefix and nothing from them reaches the token block. The remaining 146 carry
Nulab's own names, and the per-product families (`--button-backlog-*`, `--banner-cacoo-*`)
could not come from a generic theme.

### One focus ring, two brands, two properties

`--outline-primary` `#cbc2e8` renders on six controls across both domains. On filled buttons it
is an `outline: … solid 4px`; on the two quiet controls it is a `box-shadow: … 0 0 0 4px`. Same
colour, same 4px, different property by control weight.

`solid 4px` settles the authorship question: Chrome's own `:focus-visible` ring is
`auto 1px`, and a control page carrying no author stylesheet paints exactly that
(`docs/MEASUREMENT_METHOD_2026-09-22.md` §2.5). Nothing here reports `auto`.

The neutral button needed a second reading. In the handle-pinned pass its `:hover` matched
false, so hover came back unchanged; re-run through `probe-component-states.mjs`, which loads a
fresh page per state, it returns `rgb(105, 106, 125)`. The recorded hover is that reading.

## The Backlog colours — two readings, and why this is not a `conflicts` entry

The corporate sheet gives Backlog **two greens** — `--brand-backlog` `#42ce9f` for the mark and
`--button-backlog` `#319b77` for actions. `backlog.com`'s own buttons are **`#f27100` orange**,
which appears nowhere in the corporate 146.

It was first written into `verification_v2.conflicts` and then taken back out, because it is
not one. A conflict in this schema is **two sources disagreeing about one claim**. These are
two correct measurements of two different things: what the corporate stylesheet names as
Backlog's button token, and what Backlog's own site paints on its buttons. Nothing contradicts
anything.

Both readings are kept where they were taken — the greens in the palette, the orange on the
Backlog components — and the difference is stated in §2, §4 and §7 of the reference. Listing it
as a conflict would have blocked the tier for a disagreement that does not exist, and the
prose carries the caution better than the field would.

`--typetalk-primary`, `-secondary`, `-background`, `-background-light`, `--banner-typetalk`,
`--brand-typetalk` and the four `--button-typetalk-*` are all present and resolving. Typetalk
is **not** among the products Nulab's own About page lists (Backlog, Cacoo, Nulab Pass,
Flowbase). The family is recorded as measured; no claim is made about the product's status.

## Tier 2 cross-checks

getdesign.md and refero.design return no Nulab record. Both token sources are Nulab-owned
(`nulab.com` and `backlog.com`), satisfying the two-brand-owned-source rule for JP.

## Evidence limitations

- **Two marketing surfaces.** None of the four products was opened; the in-product UI is
  unmeasured, and the per-product button families are read from the sheet, not from rendered
  product buttons — only `--button-default` and `--button-nulab` were seen rendering.
- **No shadow token.** Two shadows were observed and neither is named.
- **No easing token.** Four different transition declarations were observed, all ad hoc.
- **No breakpoint measured.** The layout widths are declared; the behaviour is not recorded.
- `rgb(106,106,106)`, the most common rendered text colour, is not in the token set and is not
  promoted to one.
- Desktop 1440×1000, light scheme, logged out.
