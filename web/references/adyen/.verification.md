## Scope and collector boundary

### Two collectors

A delegated subagent (Sonnet, headless only) ran the deep probe and read both header controls as
"no hover change". Re-measured independently:

- Contact us: the element's own values do not change on hover, but its `::before` goes from
  `opacity: 0` to `opacity: 1` with `background-color: oklab(0.1755 -0.019 -0.039 / 0.074)` — navy
  `#001222` at 7.4%, the value of `--main-light-secondary` (`:hover` true). Focus:
  `outline: rgb(255,255,255) solid 1px`.
- Pricing: no change on hover, pseudo-elements included; focus the same 1px white outline.

**Tool defect found here:** `probe-component-states.mjs` did not read pseudo-elements, so this
hover read as unchanged. The tool now compares `::before`/`::after`, and a sweep of committed
"no change" hovers for pseudo overlays is running.

The subagent's mega-menu trigger reading hung and is not used.
