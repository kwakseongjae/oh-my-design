# pixiv — Verification Notes (2026-09-21)

Pipeline: live re-measure of an existing `legacy_snapshot` reference · Brand: pixiv (ピクシブ)

## Scope and collector boundary

- Measured directly with Playwright-driven Chrome (1440×900, light and dark forced), reading
  `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement)` on the live page. No stored collector bundle
  was used; `artifacts/reference-evidence/` holds no pixiv capture.
- Two evidence domains, kept separate:
  - **Product surface** — `https://www.pixiv.net/` (logged-out home). Establishes that pixiv's
    published design system resolves on pixiv's own production origin, and supplies every
    colour and typography token.
  - **Component surface** — `https://charcoal-web.pixiv.design/` story frames. Supplies
    component geometry and state deltas only.
- The logged-out home renders a **sign-in wall** (≈180 characters of visible text). This does
  not weaken the token evidence — the stylesheet is the product's own and 328 `--charcoal-*`
  properties resolve — but it means no page-level layout, grid or navigation claim is made
  from it.

  `win-bug-omega, system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Ubuntu, Cantarell, "Noto Sans", "Hiragino Kaku Gothic ProN", Meiryo, sans-serif`.

## Source-domain separation

| Domain | Sources | What it supports | What it does not support |
|---|---|---|---|
| Live product surface | `pixiv.net` home, light + dark | Every colour and typography token; that Charcoal ships on pixiv's own origin | Components, states, layout, grid — the page is a sign-in wall |
| Published component library | `charcoal-web.pixiv.design` story frames | Component geometry and hover/press/focus deltas | Which components pixiv.net actually composes, or their production density |
| Official repository | `github.com/pixiv/charcoal` | That Charcoal is pixiv's own system, Apache-2.0 | Current computed values |

## Earlier values removed or bounded

- The previous pass declared 24 colours as `prose-derived`. Three of them — `#4caf50`,
  `#ff9800`, `#ffb300` — are **Material Design palette values** (Green 500, Orange 500,
  Amber 600), and a ten-step generic grey ramp (`#fafafa` … `#333333`) has no counterpart in
  Charcoal. These are removed at the field boundary, not approximated to a nearby token.
- `primary-tint #e3f3ff`, `dark-raised #2b2b2b` and `mono: SF Mono` have no measured
  counterpart and are removed.
- Secondary/outline, follow-toggle and premium button variants are removed: no published
  story matches them. No disabled state was captured for any component.
- The five-level shadow table in §6 is retained as prose but explicitly marked unmeasured;
  the only shadow properties on the page are Tailwind's.

## Evidence limitations

- The home page is a sign-in wall. Layout, grid, breakpoint and navigation claims in §5 and
  §8 remain earlier-pass prose and are **not** covered by this verification.
- Component evidence comes from the published library, not from pixiv.net's own DOM; it shows
  what Charcoal ships, not which parts the product composes.
- No authenticated surface, no mobile viewport, no motion or transition timing was captured.
- Tier 2 cross-checks: getdesign.md and refero.design return no pixiv record. JP Tier 2
  coverage is absent as a rule, which is why the JP regional Tier-1 ≥ 2 requirement applies.
