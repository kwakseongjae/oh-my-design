## Scope and source domains

Every value below was read from a surface Mitsubishi Electric's DX Innovation Center
publishes, measured with playwright driving a real Chrome at 1440x900 with the colour
scheme forced to light, on 2026-09-21. No collector bundle was supplied and none was
reused - this is a first capture.

- `serendie.design` and its Foundations/Components pages - the design system itself.
- `github.com/serendie` - the organisation, whose own description reads "An
  open-source design system by Mitsubishi Electric". Ownership was confirmed from the
  rendered organisation identity, not from a status code.
- `mitsubishielectric.co.jp/serendie/` - the Serendie platform business site, which
  establishes that Serendie names a product existing apart from the design system.
  That is what admits this reference under the library/brand rule in AGENTS.md.

**What this reference does not cover.** Serendie Design System is deliberately
skinnable: its About page describes a dress-up design and states that business
domains add custom themes overriding Serendie UI. Everything recorded is the default
KONJO theme, the Serendie brand's own expression. It is not Mitsubishi Electric's
corporate identity and not a description of every Mitsubishi product.

### Measurement corrections made during this pass

Two readings were taken and discarded before the ones above.

1. The first state capture read hover, press and focus without moving the pointer
   away, so the hover fill would have been recorded as the focus value. The pointer
   is parked between reads in the measurement that stands, and focus is consequently
   **not** asserted - it was not separable from hover on the observed component.
2. The buttons first targeted - "Filled", "Outlined", "Ghost" - are the documentation
   page's own tab chrome, not component demos. Their 48px height and 14px type are the
   docs UI. The recorded component is the KONJO theme action.
