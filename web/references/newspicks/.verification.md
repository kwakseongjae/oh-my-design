## Scope and collector boundary

- Local Playwright-driven Chrome (Tier 1), 1440×1100, `ja-JP` with a matching
  `accept-language`, light scheme forced, `getComputedStyle` on the document element and on

## Namespace ownership and the nonsense-path control

`https://newspicks.com/zz-this-does-not-exist` returns a real **HTTP 404** titled
`404 Not Found` with **89 characters** of body. Not a catch-all.

**All 220 custom properties are NewsPicks'.** No framework prefix appears at the document
element — no `--tw-`, `--wp-`, `--el-`, `--bs-`, `--mantine-`, and no `oklch` shadcn layer.
This is unusual enough to state plainly: nothing had to be separated out, which is the
opposite of jal (63 `--tw-*` plus 31 oklch), nulab (60 WordPress) and chatwork (an embedded
Marketo form).

Namespaces: `--palette-*` 84 · `--surface-color-*` 53 · `--text-color-*` 22 ·
`--object-color-*` 19 · `--border-color-*` 19 · `--premium-*` 7 · `--brand-*` 6 ·
`--third-party-color-*` 5 · plus `--body-color-*`, `--unread-color-*`, `--gradient-color-*`.

### The selected tab's label is white, and the element says otherwise

The selected tab computes `color: rgb(34, 34, 34)` on a `rgb(20, 106, 245)` fill, which read
naively is dark ink on a saturated blue — a contrast failure worth reporting. It is not one.
The element's own `color` is inherited and unused; **its child `span` computes
`rgb(255, 255, 255)`**, which is what renders.

Checked by walking the control's children rather than trusting its own computed style. The
component records `fg: "#ffffff"`.

### Focus

Every focusable control computes `outline: rgb(0, 95, 204) auto 1px` — Chrome's default ring
(`docs/MEASUREMENT_METHOD_2026-09-22.md` §2.5). The `auto` style is the tell. **No focus token
is recorded**, and none exists in the set.

## Tier 2 cross-checks

getdesign.md and refero.design return no NewsPicks record. The token source and the control
are both NewsPicks-owned.

**One brand-owned surface only.** The JP regional rule wants two; the second source here is
the nonsense-path control on the same host, which establishes the route is real but is not an
independent surface. This is recorded as a limitation rather than papered over — a second
NewsPicks-owned surface (the corporate site, or an article route) would strengthen it and was
not measured.

## Evidence limitations

- **One measured surface**, and it is the logged-out public feed. NewsPicks is partly
  paywalled; the Premium experience, the comment composer and the article reader were not
  measured.
- **No type token exists.** The sizes in the reference are rendered readings, not declarations,
  and are labelled as such.
- **No font family, no shadow, no motion token.** All three absent from the set; the two
  transition values observed are inline and differ by control.
- **Disabled is thoroughly tokenised and was not observed rendering**, so no disabled component
  is declared.
- `grey` appears twice as a literal CSS keyword and is not promoted.
- Desktop 1440×1100, light scheme, logged out.
