## 2026-09-21 — the design system was found, and the June values held

**What changed.** `storybook.asana.com` was discovered by the surface sweep
(`docs/SURFACE_SWEEP_2026-09-21.md`); nothing in this reference had cited it. It publishes
**526 stories across 215 components**, including `Design System` stories for Colors,
Typography, Spacing and Shadows. It is now recorded as `ds` and cited as Tier-1.

**Same design system, two surfaces.** `asana.com` itself resolves the **same 353 CSS custom
properties** the Storybook does, so tokens are claimed from the product surface rather than
only from documentation. `--lightmode-*` (62) and `--darkmode-*` (62) both resolve: Asana
ships a full dark theme, which this pass did **not** measure and does not claim.

**Every button hover is a step on the published `black` ramp** — `black 600`, `black 100`,
`black 50`, `black 20`. The hover contract is a move along the neutral ramp, not a per-button colour.

## Evidence limitations — 2026-09-21

- **Two of eight interactive components carry no observed state**, so this reference stays
  `partial` rather than `verified_v2`: `button-hero-accent` (absent from today's surfaces) and
  `input-default` (its surface is the authenticated login, not re-measured). That is the honest
  blocker, not a formatting gap.
- The dark theme is published (`--darkmode-*`, 62 properties) and entirely unclaimed.
- Component evidence comes from marketing and pricing surfaces. Asana's authenticated product
  app was not measured, and none of these tokens are claimed for it.
