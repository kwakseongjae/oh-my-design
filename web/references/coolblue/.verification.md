## Scope and collector boundary

- **Collector: Aside** (the owner's Chrome, via `mcp__aside__repl`). coolblue.nl returns a 403
  challenge page to headless Chrome (three attempts by the delegated probe); plain HTTP fetches of
  its text pages work.
- **Privacy:** one fresh tab via `openTab()`, closed at the end; no existing tabs listed or
  attached. `looksAuthenticated` checked before measuring: **false**. Only computed styles and
  geometry were read; controls were marked in-page by colour, and the consent dialog's button labels
  were read only to find a non-accepting option. No sign-in, basket or form.
- **Consent:** the dialog offers "Alles accepteren" and "Zelf instellen". Nothing was accepted:
  "Zelf instellen" was opened and its panel closed with "Sluiten". The green button measured as the
  action style is the dialog's accept button — its states were read by hovering and focusing, never
  clicking.
- **Reproduce:** Aside or any browser the site does not challenge; desktop, nl-NL.
- Desktop only. Captured and authored 2026-09-26.

### Two collectors

measurements here are the main session's. `AdobeClean` fonts in `document.fonts` come from the
browser environment (they also appeared on another site read through Aside) and are excluded.
