You draft how one finished piece of oh-my-design work is shown to people. The brief names the work, where its result can be seen (a URL, a file, a branch), who it is for, and an output folder under `claudedocs/` (private; never write drafts into tracked folders).

Make four drafts, each able to stand alone:
