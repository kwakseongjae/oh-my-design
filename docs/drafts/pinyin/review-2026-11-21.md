Remarks from the model's own knowledge of the language. These have no source, are not rules, and are here so that a later reader knows where to look first:
- PW-01 (你, not 您): the four company guides are consumer and B2B product teams. Banking, insurance and government services in the Mainland write 您 almost without exception; Teambition's exception says as much. A project in those fields should set this in its DESIGN.md before the check is read.
- PW-16: 登陆 for 登录 and 帐号 for 账号 are corrections most editors would make. 其它 is different: it is a standard word (for things), the guides prefer 其他 for consistency, and Ant's own page writes 其它 in its running text (see below).
- PW-06: 唯一 and 最佳 have neutral technical uses (唯一标识, 最佳实践). The check will prompt on them.
- PW-07: 不要 and 请勿 are the ordinary words of any instruction. The rule is about commanding the user; the check cannot see who is addressed.
- PW-38 (no 、 between consecutive “” or 《》): MIIT's own document writes ”、“ (see below). The national standard, as far as the model knows it, says such marks "usually" take no 、. FYI fits.
- PW-55 (周一, not 星期一): one company's house style. 星期一 is fully standard.
- PW-59 (a space between Han and Latin or digits): the practice of tech-company style guides. Mainland publishing and government text do not space. The check reads running text only, which keeps it out of labels.
