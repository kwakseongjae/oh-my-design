# Quotes checked against the served bytes

Made by `scripts/lang/check-quotes-raw.mjs` on 2026-11-21 from `writing/rules.md`.

- A tier says where the characters of a quote are. It never says the candidate reads the passage correctly; that is `verdicts.md`.
- Only "web" confirms both the characters and the spaces of a quote in a source that is current. A width or spacing rule may rest on a "web" quote and on no other tier.
- Only 「…」 quotes of four or more characters are read. Single marks and English-language quotes are not tested, so a source quoted only in English shows 0.
- A quote counts as found if any listed source holds it; the tables name which.
- Nobody who reads the language natively has seen this.
