# /kana writing, second pass: second-reader verdicts on rules.md (UW-58 … UW-70 and additions)

Checked 2026-11-21 against `rules.md`, `sources.json` and `gaps.md` in this directory. Region for every rule: Japan; every source is written in or for Japanese as used in Japan, so no rule is "wrong region". This file gives verdicts only; it repairs nothing.

## How this check was done, and its limit

- **Microsoft (MS)**: read from `/Users/kwakseongjae/.omd-workers/kana/sources/ms-ja-style-2026-11-21.txt` by script. Quotes were matched after removing only line breaks and indentation; 27 quotes with a line citation matched by script within the cited lines (±2), and the remaining ones (side-by-side table cells, and quotes that themselves contain quotation marks) were read at the cited lines by eye. None was missing or different. The PDF itself was not opened (no tool for it here), so nothing below says what the PDF shows beyond its text layer. Negative claims checked by script: the extraction has no 円, 通貨, "currency", はい／いいえ, "empty", お願い, U+00A5, U+00A0 or U+3000.
- **Web pages**: the only fetch tool here passes the page through an extraction model; no shell, no page source. The nine pages cited in this pass returned HTTP 200; the first-file pages opened for the other sides also opened and returned text. Passages were pulled blind (by heading or a short keyword, never by showing the rule's quote), twice each, and the rule file's quotes were then compared by script with what came back. This is not a byte-level check.
- **Code points** from web pages are those of the strings the extraction model returned. They agreed across two pulls, but they are model-mediated: read them as "reported", not verified.
- **Opened for the other side of each disagreement**: WordPress style guide, Mozilla L10n Guideline, mozilla-japan Editorial Guideline wiki, SmartHR writing-style and app-writing, Ubie UX writing, Ubie Button, Spindle UIラベリング and button, SmartHR count page, DADS button usage. **Not re-opened**: KB (the JW-07 quote in UW-58), JW-61, JW-35/JTF, DADS-md, OPT; statements in rules.md that lean on them are not covered by these verdicts.
- **Count**: the brief speaks of 12 additions; rules.md has 8 "Adds to" entries (UW-06, 09, 10, 15, 16, 17, 36, 37) plus a line "UW-08: no addition". 13 candidates + 8 additions = 21 rows.
