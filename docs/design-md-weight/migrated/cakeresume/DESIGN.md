### Principles

## 5. Layout & Platforms

The following layout-rhythm and whitespace-first readings — generous-white-ground, whitespace-as-the-primary-structuring-tool, shared-32px-height-along-a-shared-baseline, uniform-4px-across-buttons-selector-and-chips, hierarchy-by-scale-and-color-rather-than-heavy-dividers, quiet-`#D1D6D4`-borders-only-where-structure-needs-an-edge, and one-decisive-green-action-per-region — are a derived editorial implementation inference from the verified surfaces; they are not Cake-authored or a separately published UI specification. Cake composes on a generous white ground where whitespace is the primary structuring tool. Controls share a consistent 32px height, so buttons, the language selector, and adjacent fields align along a shared baseline. The small 4px radius is applied uniformly across buttons, the selector, and chips. Hierarchy is carried by scale and color rather than by heavy dividers — a 38px deep-green heading sets the top of a view, 16px body fills the reading column, and quiet `#D1D6D4` borders mark only where structure needs an edge. Keep one decisive green action per region.

The following responsive reading — 16px-body-remains-the-reading-baseline, 38px-heading-scales-down-so-it-never-crowds-the-column, header-selector-and-32px-actions-collapse-while-keeping-4px-and-shared-height, single-column-reflow-because-fills-and-borders-not-fixed-width-shadows, and primary-green-stays-prominent-and-reachable-at-every-size — is a derived editorial implementation inference from the verified surfaces; it is not Cake-authored or a separately published UI specification. The source states that the 16px body remains the reading baseline, and that the 38px deep-green heading scales down as viewport width tightens so it never crowds the column. The header’s language selector and the 32px-tall action buttons collapse into a compact arrangement on narrow screens while keeping 4px corners and shared height. Because separation relies on `#E2E6E4` fills and 1px `#D1D6D4` borders rather than fixed-width shadows, components reflow into a single column. The primary green action stays prominent and reachable at every size.
