### Scope

Gaudiy (株式会社Gaudiy Group) is a Tokyo Web3 fan-community company. The source records its flagship product as Gaudiy Fanlink, a platform that lets IP and entertainment brands run digital fan experiences on blockchain rails; that product description belongs to the source's brand narrative in the final paragraph of this section, not to the interface evidence, and its evidence classes are separated there. This contract covers the two first-party public web surfaces the source inspected live on 2026-06-17: the corporate site at `https://gaudiy.com/` and the recruit special site at `https://special.gaudiy.com/pre-series-c/recruit` (reached through a `recruit.gaudiy.com` redirect). Values stay attached to the surface that established them. Neither surface is treated as a proxy for the Gaudiy Fanlink product itself, for a mobile app, or for the Tech Blog, Gaudiy AI Lab, Member note, and CEO'S note destinations the navigation points at.

The inspected interface layer is monochrome. The canvas is pure white (`#ffffff`); text is set not in pure black but in a softened charcoal (`#333333`, 223–285 occurrences live); structure is drawn with hard, sharp-cornered black borders (`#050505`, `#000000`) rather than soft cards or shadows. The source's accent scan records no saturated hue on either surface. Emphasis is generated through contrast mechanics: black-on-white inversion, heavy 1–2px black rules, and 0px corners everywhere except one control. Headlines run in Noto Sans JP Bold (weight 700) at 64px on the corporate hero ("ファンと共に、時代を進める。" / "With fans, advance the era.") with `-3.2px` negative tracking and an 89.6px (1.40) line height. Section heads drop to 28px / weight 700 at a 39.2px (1.40) line height but flip to **positive** `+1.6px` tracking ("ファン国家の創造", "たぶんチャンス。"). Body and UI text fall back to a plain 16px / weight 400 sans-serif (Noto Sans / Noto Sans JP) at a 1.50 ratio. Live inspection found `box-shadow: none` across the hero, nav, cards, and CTAs on both surfaces. Separation comes from flat grey section bands (`#eeeeee`) and black borders — 1px (`#000000`) on corporate news cards, a heavier 2px (`#050505`) on the recruit special site's job and CTA cards. The muted greys (`#555555` on recruit, `#777777` on corporate, `#656565` faint) form a quiet neutral ladder for secondary text.

### Distinctive traits

### Principles

These five items are a derived editorial implementation inference from the verified surfaces; they are not Gaudiy-authored or a separately published UI specification. The source states them in its own Principles section together with the UI implication it draws from each, and its closing note marks readings of this kind — "monochrome as conviction" among them — as editorial.

1. **Fans as co-creators, not consumers.** The "ファン国家の創造" vision treats fandom as participatory. *UI implication:* surfaces should invite contribution and belonging, not just transaction; community and creation come before commerce.
2. **Seriousness over hype.** Gaudiy rejects Web3's hype register in both copy and design. *UI implication:* state facts plainly (partnership and funding headlines with dates), avoid superlatives and urgency; let restraint signal credibility.
3. **Monochrome as conviction.** The colorless palette is a deliberate stance against glossy crypto branding. *UI implication:* never reach for an accent hue to create emphasis — use black-on-white inversion and weight instead.
4. **Structure you can see.** Hard black borders make the layout legible and graphic. *UI implication:* draw containers and CTAs with visible 1–2px black rules and 0px corners; structure should read like an editorial grid.
5. **Flat, fast, and graphic.** No shadows, no elevation, no decoration. *UI implication:* separate with grey bands and rules; keep the page light, sharp, and quick to scan.

### Application rules

The rules the source states as its Do list, kept as written, plus one restraint rule the source states only inside its removed tool-facing section. These rules, and the reasons attached to them, are a derived editorial implementation inference from the verified surfaces; they are not Gaudiy-authored or a separately published UI specification.

- Keep the palette monochrome — black (`#000000` / `#050505`), white (`#ffffff`), and grey (`#eeeeee`) only
- Set body and headings in charcoal `#333333`, not pure black
- Use Noto Sans JP Bold (700) for Japanese display headlines
- Apply tight negative tracking on the hero (`-3.2px` at 64px) and positive tracking on section heads (`+1.6px` at 28px)
- Draw structure with sharp-cornered black borders — 1px on corporate, 2px (`#050505`) on recruit
- Keep 0px radius on every button, card, and band
- Use black-on-white inversion (solid `#000000` fill, white text) for emphasis instead of an accent color
- Separate sections with flat grey (`#eeeeee`) bands, never shadows
- Use the solid black inversion CTA sparingly, for the single primary action

### Avoid

The source states these as its Don't list, kept as written. These prohibitions, and the reasons attached to them, are a derived editorial implementation inference from the verified surfaces; they are not Gaudiy-authored or a separately published UI specification.

- Introduce a saturated accent hue — the system is intentionally colorless
- Round corners on buttons, cards, or bands — sharpness is the brand
- Use drop shadows for elevation — Gaudiy is flat and shadow-free
- Use pure black (`#000000`) for body text — reserve it for borders and inversion; text is `#333333`
- Soften the hero with a light weight — display is always Bold (700)
- Drop the positive tracking on section heads — the spaced-out label cadence is deliberate
- Reach for gradients or glossy fills — the identity is graphic and flat
- Mix display fonts — Noto Sans JP owns Japanese headlines; sans-serif owns body

### Semantic color

Ink & text:

- **Border Ink** (`#050505`): the structural black used for the heavy 2px borders on the recruit special site's cards and CTAs. The system's `primary_color` — the color that draws every box.
- **Pure Black** (`#000000`): 1px corporate news-card borders, solid-black inline CTAs, and inversion blocks.
- **Charcoal Text** (`#333333`): the dominant text color (223–285 occurrences live) — headings, body, nav, links. Softened black for warmth and readability.
- **Muted Slate** (`#555555`): secondary text and captions on the recruit site.
- **Muted Alt** (`#777777`): tertiary / fine-print grey on the corporate site.
- **Faint Grey** (`#656565`): lowest-emphasis labels and metadata.

Surface:

- **Pure White** (`#ffffff`): page background, card surfaces, and the text color on inverted black elements.
- **Section Grey** (`#eeeeee`): full-width grey bands that segment the long-scroll corporate page — the only non-white surface.

Inversion:

- **On Ink** (`#ffffff`): white text and icons on solid-black CTAs, close buttons, and inverted bands. The system's emphasis mechanic is inversion, not color.

The role names above and the readings attached to them — charcoal as a softened black chosen for warmth and readability, the border ink as the color that draws every box, and inversion rather than color as the system's emphasis mechanic — are a derived editorial implementation inference from the verified surfaces; they are not Gaudiy-authored or a separately published UI specification. ### Elevation

| Level | Treatment | Use |

| Tint (Level 1) | `#eeeeee` grey band | Section separation without elevation |

Motion rules as the source states them: motion is sharp and functional, matching the graphic, flat aesthetic. The signature interaction is the **inversion flip** — a CTA or label snapping from white-with-black-border to solid-black-with-white-text on hover/press at `motion-fast`. Cards and sections fade in from below at `motion-standard / ease-enter`. In keeping with the brand's "glitch" concept, brief, intentional glitch/cut transitions may punctuate hero reveals, while everyday UI motion stays restrained. There is no bounce and no spring — the identity is described as signalling conviction and steadiness rather than playful delight. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, all transitions collapse to instant and any glitch effect is suppressed; the product remains fully functional.

Official documentation of a single curve or duration is not that gate. Any exact animation curve remains a local extension until that per-component computed observation exists.
