# Acceptance-debt and fit-margin repair — 1.9.518

The satellite transfer failed after a compact candidate had already passed selector provenance and all overflow/clipping checks. It planned to the 8px measurement threshold itself, leaving only 5.421875px at 320px, and it carried a known 4.31:1 muted-text contrast failure through the edit. Both failures were visible before delivery, but neither was a machine-bound obligation in the artifact.

The schema 0.3 artifact now requires an `acceptance_debt_ledger` before the first product edit. Each debt names its gate, selector, baseline evidence, correction, outcome, proof mode, and immutable static guardrail. Browser-measured debts must bind to pre-edit row groups; fail-close debts cannot impersonate browser proof. Every debt assertion must also exist in the global static closure manifest, and unresolved debt blocks closure.

Fit planning now uses a 16px reserve while independent acceptance still measures the original 8px threshold. This separates design margin from the pass line and avoids optimizing directly against a brittle boundary. The repair adds no task selector, product fact, color, or benchmark answer and made no provider call.
