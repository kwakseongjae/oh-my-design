The table

How a seat works.

A cupping is a sequence. We keep it short and quiet. You keep your notes.

  1. 1

    Sit

    Your place is set. Phones stay off the table. We tell you the origin, process, and roast date — not a score.

  2. 2

    Fragrance

    Smell the dry ground. Write what you get. There is no right answer on our sheet.

  3. 3

    Break

    Hot water, a crust, then the break. Steam, then liquor.

  4. 4

    Notes

    You taste and write. We do not collect, average, or publish what you mark.
