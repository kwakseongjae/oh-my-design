How a sitting works

Sit the table we use to evaluate a roast

Bowls are set in a line. Lots are poured. The room stays quiet enough to talk about what is actually in the cup. A seat is a place in that line, not a cafe table and not a tasting flight with a published menu.

We have not published a calendar, a score sheet, or a price. If you want the next sitting, open the reservation slip with a date and how many chairs you need. The slip stays in this browser; nothing is sent.
