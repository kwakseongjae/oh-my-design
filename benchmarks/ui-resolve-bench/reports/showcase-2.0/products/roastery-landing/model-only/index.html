Kiln Annex is a small-lot roastery. The photograph is the room: a drum roaster, morning light, and green coffee in burlap. Cupping seats are reserved so the table stays small.

No price, award, or score is listed on this page. The table is where those notes, if any, are spoken.

The lot

What is on the floor is what we roast.

The sacks in the roast-room photograph are marked Guatemala SHB, Ethiopia, and Colombia. That is the coffee on the floor in that picture — not a catalog of every origin we might ever buy.

Lot prices, farm awards, and cupping scores are not published here. If those figures exist for a given bag, they belong at the table, not in a badge row.

02

The pour

A cupping seat is a quiet tray, not a class.

You sit with the current roast, water at the right heat, and time to write. It is not a ticketed seminar and not a flight with a printed fee. We have not published a public class calendar on this page.

Seats stay few so the tray stays small. That is the only capacity rule we state here.
