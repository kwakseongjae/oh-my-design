The work

What this roastery is

We roast in the room you can see. What we do not publish — prices, scores, hours — is listed further down, unvarnished.

The seat

Two chairs, one table

This is the room a reserved seat occupies. Capacity is small; that is why the only action on this page is a request, not a ticket.

A reserved seat

What you are reserving

A cupping seat is a timed place at the table for one to six people. It is not a walk-up bar ticket and it is not a class with a published syllabus.

  1. 01

    You request a window

    Choose a date and a morning or afternoon window. Exact clock times are not published; we treat the window as a preference.

  2. 02

    The request stays here

    There is no booking desk or confirmation email on this page. Submitting stores the request on this device only.

  3. 03

    The table is the lot of the day

    What is poured is whatever has come off the drum. We do not list a rotating menu, origin scores, or a fee.

Before you request

Tell us a seat count and any access need in the notes field. We have not published allergen sheets, a children’s policy, or a cancellation rule.

If you need a written confirmation, that path is not on this site. The form will say so again when you submit.

Public record

What is not published

This page does not invent the missing pieces. If a fact is not below as “not published,” we have not stated it.

  • Prices Cupping fees, retail bags, and wholesale rates are not listed.
  • Awards No competition placements, medals, or “best of” marks are claimed.
  • Review scores No star ratings, critic quotes, or numbered cupping scores appear here.
  • Partners No café, importer, or equipment logos are shown or implied.
  • Hours and address Public opening hours and a street address are not published on this page.
  • Confirmation channel No phone, inbox, or booking desk is published. Requests are not transmitted.
