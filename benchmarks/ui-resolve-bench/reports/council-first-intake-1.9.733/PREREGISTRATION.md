inventing product facts: explicit user intent and grounded reversible process
choices are automatic, authority-bearing product decisions are interviewed,
and generic or unknown visual defaults are deferred.

- installed helper parity across Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, and Cursor;

2. ambiguous audience/scope/CTA remains `interview`;
3. a generic greenfield wow fallback is `defer`, never `auto`;
4. six future lanes are declared read-only and `not-dispatched`;
5. installer, focused tests, type-check, build, and frontier audit pass.

## Guardrails
