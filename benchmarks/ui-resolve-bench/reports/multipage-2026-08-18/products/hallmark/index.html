Books, then coffee.

Quire is a one-room bookshop that also pours coffee. The chairs face the window. The shelves are arranged by how a book sits in the hand, not by how fast you can find it.

We keep a short fiction wall, a poetry ledge, and a stack of used cookbooks by the grinder. The café is the same counter as the till. Cup prices are not published — ask when you order.

What stays on the ledge

New books arrive on Thursdays. We write the talkers by hand and move them when a title has said enough. There is no staff-picks carousel. If you want a recommendation, stand still until someone looks up.
