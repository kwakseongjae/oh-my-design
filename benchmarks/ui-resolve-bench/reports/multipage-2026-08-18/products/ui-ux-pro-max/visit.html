02 — The bar

Two cups and a case that stays quiet.

Marble, ceramic, a pastry case held just out of focus so the conversation can stay on the page. The bar sits at the back of the house in this sample, behind the tall shelves.

If you came looking for a menu board transcribed onto the web: café prices are not published on this site.

03 — The shelves

Spines in a muted register.

The browse is the main room: paper shelf-talkers, a nook that keeps the afternoon, and a ladder that is more furniture than tool. Nothing here is a stock list.

Book prices are not published on this site. The sample events — including Moth Paper Letters — use the same floor.
