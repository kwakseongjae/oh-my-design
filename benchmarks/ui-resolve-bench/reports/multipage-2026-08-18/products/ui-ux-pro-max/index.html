01 — The house

Shelves first. The bar keeps them company.

Quire House is written as an independent bookstore-café: new titles beside well-kept used copies, paper shelf-talkers, and a marble counter that does not rush you. This site is a sample shopfront, not a live booking desk.

We do not publish a street address, opening hours, or prices. Those details belong on the floor of a real shop; they are not listed here.

Bring a book you have not finished. We will keep the table.
02 — Three rooms

What the house holds

The same three rooms, described without a catalogue or a tariff.

  • The shelves

    A slow browse

    Fiction, essays, and the odd well-made cookbook. Staff notes are written on paper, not screens. Book prices are not published on this site.

  • The bar

    A second cup

    Espresso, tea, and a pastry case kept a little out of focus so the books stay loud. Menu prices are not published on this site.

  • The room

    A table for eight

    Readings, a binding table, a club that argues kindly. The sample programme lives on the events page.
