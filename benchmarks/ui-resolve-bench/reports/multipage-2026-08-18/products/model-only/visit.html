Inside

The floor

Shelves, marble, lamp

One room. The long wall holds clothbound fiction and a few stubborn paperbacks. Staff notes — sample ones — sit in the spines. The bar is marble; the pastry case is short; the espresso machine faces the window so the afternoon does some of the work.

The bar

Coffee, a short kitchen

Espresso, tea, and whatever came out of the oven that morning. We do not print a menu on this page.

Menu prices are not published on this site.
