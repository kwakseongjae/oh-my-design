The book

A novel of weather and accounts

The Salt Ledger is a sample title. In the story we sketched for this page, a clerk on a cold coast keeps two books: one for the harbour, one for the weather she is not supposed to notice.

Ellison — a fictional guest — would read a short passage, take questions, and sign the shop copies. There is no living author attached to this name.

“I kept the second book in the same hand, so the numbers would forgive me.”

Invented line, used here as a pull quote.
