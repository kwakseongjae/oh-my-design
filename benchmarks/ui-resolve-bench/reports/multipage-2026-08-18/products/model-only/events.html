How to read this list

Nothing here is on sale and nobody named will appear. The three listings exist so the shop can show its voice: a kicker, a title, a photograph, and an honest line about money.

Ticket prices, workshop fees, and any door charge are not published on this site. Opening hours for the evening of an event are not published here either — they would be stated on the visit page, where they are also unpublished.
