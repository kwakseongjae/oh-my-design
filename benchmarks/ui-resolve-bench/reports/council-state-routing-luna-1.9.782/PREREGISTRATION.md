Task, prompt, starter, DESIGN.md, installed OmD skill bytes, Luna/high runtime, timeout, browser socket contract, evaluator, and deterministic reflow lifecycle are equal. - 720-second timeout, 30-second inter-cell pacing, no retry, replacement, fallback, repair, or model substitution.
- Exact local browser attachment `omd1782` at `http://127.0.0.1:9352`; the runner may not launch a browser.

## Acceptance

Each arm requires Reliability@3: all three cells must score 85/85, be UI-Resolved, pass proof compliance, use one product revision and one successful static closure, preserve sealed inventory, and promote exact candidate bytes. Recovery, duplicate closure, post-ready verification, passive protected-text scrolling, and unplanned user questions must remain zero.

The paired candidate may not lose objective points. Registered questions, unplanned questions, authority preservation, user handoffs, wall time, and token components are reported separately. External-evidence blockers are never counted as interviews. This sample alone cannot promote 2.0 or establish general council lift.
