## Primary question

On the same fresh existing-surface repair and the same Cursor/Grok 4.5 High
runtime, does a provider-sealed zero-dispatch maintenance ledger preserve full
task quality without adding material execution cost or prompting the user?

## Frozen arms

- **off:** current `omd:apply`, no council intake packet;
- **on:** byte-identical `omd:apply` plus a provider-sealed council ledger and
  dispatch plan produced before model exposure.

The on arm is valid only when the deterministic plan has zero selected lanes,
zero model-lane calls, and zero interview questions. It may preserve typed
`defer` decisions but must not promote values.

## Controls

- task: `oceanographic-cast-custody-v0.1`;
- runtime/model: Cursor / Grok 4.5 High;
- effort: high; timeout: 720 seconds;
- concurrency: one; retry/replacement: zero;
- fixed order: off, then on;
- same prompt core, starter, DESIGN.md, source contract, evaluator epoch, and
  product proof contract.

## Acceptance

Both cells must finish with valid objective evidence, exact-route behavior,
accessibility and responsive gates, evidence honesty, and provider-sealed
candidate/final byte equality. The on arm must not add a user handoff or model
lane. Wall time and reported token usage remain separate descriptive axes.

This is an Internal Preview experiment. One task cannot establish general
council lift, model superiority, or the 2.0 release gate.
