# 1.9.548 — Measured fit strategy feasibility

The offshore transfer exposed a plan/implementation contradiction rather than another rendering defect. Three identifier groups were declared `stack`, but their measured intrinsic width plus the 16px planning reserve was wider than the document itself. Full-row or stack could never satisfy that contract; both arms therefore improvised local horizontal scrolling and correctly failed terminal proof.

`plan-close` now derives `fit_strategy_feasibility` for every row from the locked row and aggregate-carrier measurements. A row that cannot physically fit the document must declare an accessible named `comparison-scroll` relationship carrier before the first product edit. This prevents a model from promising stack and implementing scroll later.

The carrier rule is now precise rather than universally target-only. A decision target still requires a target-only carrier. A semantic register may share one carrier across multiple rows only when every bound row is a passive `identifier`; state, evidence, and focusable actions remain outside.

This was a provider-free repair. The frozen offshore cells were not reopened. Focused contract tests pass 46/46, TypeScript lint and diff checks pass, and the full suite passes 573 tests with the same three unrelated environment/legacy failures recorded before this change.
