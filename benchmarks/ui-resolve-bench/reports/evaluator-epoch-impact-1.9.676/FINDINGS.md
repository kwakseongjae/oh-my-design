# 1.9.676 — objective evaluator epoch boundary

Schema 0.6 introduces objective methodology epoch
`ui-resolve-objective-2026q3-passive-scroll-v1`. Future score and normalized
run records carry this value, and aggregation keys include it. Records from a
different or missing epoch cannot be silently pooled into the same system or
paired-comparison group.

## Provider-free impact audit

The local impact scanner rendered all 56 frozen task starters that explicitly
register atomic text selectors at every applicable 390px, 320px, and CSS-zoom
surrogate condition.

- audited starters: 56
- starters with protected passive text scroll: 0
- provider calls: 0
- model exposures: 0

The new rule therefore does not reclassify any frozen starter baseline. It can
change future candidate results that place `overflow:auto|scroll` directly on a
protected identifier, target, evidence, or state node. Historical score files
remain immutable and are not recomputed; their exported records stay in their
original/legacy objective epoch.

This boundary concerns deterministic objective scoring only. It does not merge
or replace Ship Preference methodology epochs, and it creates no model/skill
quality claim by itself.
