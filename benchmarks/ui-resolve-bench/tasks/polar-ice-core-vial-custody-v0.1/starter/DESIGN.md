# Polar ice-core vial custody review

## Color

- Canvas: `#EEF2F4`
- Surface: `#FCFEFF`
- Ink: `#1E2933`
- Muted text: `#64727D`
- Border: `#9DAAB3`
- Primary action: `#24516B`
- Signal: `#8A526A`

Use Ink for normal text when Muted text does not meet contrast on the actual surface. Signal communicates state and structure; it does not replace readable text.

## Typography

- Body: Arial, sans-serif
- Display: Georgia, serif
- Atomic core, archive-vial, cold-room, and accession-window identifiers remain intact. Do not abbreviate or break inside an identifier.

## Geometry

- Cards: `4px` radius
- Controls: `4px` radius

## Layout and behavior

- Preserve the core register, accession-window strip, and custody decision as three separate relationship carriers.
- Preserve five cores, seven archive-vial assignments, four accession windows, three view controls, one curator-note toggle, and one review form.
- Reflow carriers before changing typography. Do not use page-level horizontal overflow, word breaking, hidden duplicate content, or invented summaries.

## Evidence boundary

The supplied identifiers, assignments, windows, and `Archive review pending` state are observations only. They do not prove isotope analysis, core completeness, depth calibration, field provenance, vial-seal integrity, archive accession acceptance, catalog publication, or curatorial custody closure.
