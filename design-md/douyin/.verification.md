### Why no component is declared

The sign-in button — `#fe2c55`, 12px radius, 38px tall, `15px / 500` — is the only control the
logged-out surface exposes, and it **does not change on hover, press or focus**. Douyin
declares `--color-primary-hover` and `--color-secondary-hover`, so the contract exists in the
token set; this capture found nothing rendering it. The tokens are recorded as colour claims
and no component state is claimed.

### `open.douyin.com` is not a token source

The Douyin Open Platform resolves 89 properties, computes **Inter**, and paints `#1c5cfb`
(×65), `#1ff17f`, `#692cea` and `#ea9312` — a developer-portal palette with no overlap with
the product's. It is cited as the second brand-owned regional source only. This is the same
shape as `open.weibo.com` in this wave, and the same decision.

### Fonts

`tokens.typography.family` is **absent**. No font-family token appears among the 424
properties and the body stack computes `-apple-system`; a platform system face is not promoted
to a brand typeface.

## Tier 2 cross-checks

getdesign.md and refero.design return no Douyin record. Both cited sources are ByteDance-owned
Douyin domains.

## Evidence limitations

- One control observed, no state. Nothing behind the sign-in wall was measured.
- The `gift-*` and `pk-*` families are recorded as evidence of livestream surfaces this
  capture never reached; no gifting or PK component is declared.
- No shadow, elevation, transition or duration token appears among the 424 properties.
- Desktop only. Douyin is primarily a mobile application; the web surface is secondary to it.
