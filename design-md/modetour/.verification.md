## Namespace ownership and the nonsense-path control

The homepage curl control returned 200 / 396278 bytes / first title “여행은 모두투어”. The responses differ in status and length; this is not a 200 catch-all. The rendered homepage is independently status 200, final URL https://www.modetour.com/, HTML `lang="ko"`.

The corporate host’s homepage timed out in curl after 40 seconds with no bytes; its nonsense path timed out after 12 seconds with no bytes. Therefore the corporate-host catch-all comparison is unresolved. A real 85-line, dated renewal article was opened through web retrieval; no design-system or BI page is asserted on that basis. Company overview, BI, history and directions pages did not open successfully. No official design system was verified.

No Bootstrap, MUI, WordPress, Font Awesome or consent-manager namespace was recorded.

The census lists Shoplive cross-origin stylesheets and a fixed `.cloud-shoplive.csl-DraggablePipWrapper`; its styling is not used to define brand tokens. The colour census is page-wide and does not attribute every value to a DOM node, so unidentified promotional/embed colours were omitted. No inference of first-party ownership is made from an element count alone.
