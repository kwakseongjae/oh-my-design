### Country

Proposal: **UK**, category **government**. GOV.UK is a public-government website, not a commercial company with an identified Companies House registration.

- [Terms and conditions](https://www.gov.uk/help/terms-conditions), Who we are, names **“Government Digital Service”**, acting **“on behalf of the Crown”**, and says it is part of the **“Department for Science, Innovation and Technology”**. Those are the notice's operator/principal/department names; no parent company is named.
- [Privacy notice](https://www.gov.uk/help/privacy-notice) provides this legal-contact address verbatim: **“Department for Science, Innovation and Technology / 22-26 Whitehall / London / SW1A 2EG”** (line breaks represented by slashes). It is a Data Protection Officer contact address, **not labelled a registered office**. The legal notices do not supply a company registration or a registered-office field.
- [GDS's own organisation page](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/government-digital-service), Contact GDS, gives **“The White Chapel Building / 10 Whitechapel High Street / London / E1 8QS / United Kingdom”**. The [About page](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/government-digital-service/about) confirms operations across the UK and hubs in London, Manchester and Bristol. This is operational-country evidence, not a claim that every hub is a headquarters.
- **Unresolved department conflict:** the Terms and Privacy notice identify DSIT; the GDS organisation and About pages say **“Department for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport”**. No attempt is made to silently choose one as current. Both the legal and operating addresses remain in the UK, so the country proposal does not depend on resolving that hierarchy. The body identifies the operator as GDS without asserting a parent department.
