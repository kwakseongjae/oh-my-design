### Two collectors

A delegated subagent (Sonnet) ran the deep probe. It flagged both sign-up buttons as reading white
text on a white background and guessed that `--color-pairs-gradient` was unused. **Re-measured, both
were one finding**: the buttons' `background-color` is white, but `background-image` is the
`--color-pairs-gradient` PNG, and the white label sits on it. On hover the image is replaced by
`linear-gradient(… #fff, #fff, #fff)` and the label and 2px border turn `#00c1d4`. The PNG was
decoded in the page and sampled: 29×6 px, middle row at x = 0/7/14/21/28 →
`#70dfab #3bd6cb #20cfe3 #40d2e5 #46d2e5`. The login button was re-measured and matched (hover
`#a5eaf5` fill, white label).

The subagent could not isolate the header navigation (ホーム, 料金) — unmeasured, not declared. A
2019 Medium post, "Building Design System for Pairs", returned 403 and is not cited. The subagent
recorded the founding year as conflicting between sources (2008 / 2012); neither is used.
