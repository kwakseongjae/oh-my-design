### The published scale and the rendered page disagree

The thirteen-step scale is in whole pixels. The corporate page renders `15.64px`, `15.3px`,
`14.875px`, `13.8125px` — **fractional sizes from a root-relative scale**, not steps from the
token scale. Huawei publishes a thirteen-step system and its front page is on a different
footing. Recorded, not reconciled.

### Why no components are declared

Both measured controls on `consumer.huawei.com` are plain `#000000` anchors, 32px tall at 4px
radius, and **neither changes on hover, press or focus**. The corporate home's interactive
elements are 118px content cards that also do not react.

Huawei *declares* `hwp-color-brand-hover` and `hwp-color-brand-active`. This capture found
nothing rendering them. Declaring a component whose states were never observed is precisely
the error this catalog exists to prevent, so the tokens are recorded as colour claims and no
component is declared. The catalog already holds 36 verified references with no interactive
component; saying less than was measured is not available, and saying more is not allowed.

### The two surfaces do not share a system

`consumer.huawei.com` resolves **4** custom properties against the corporate site's 344, and
computes **Manrope** where the corporate site computes **Microsoft YaHei**. It is cited as the
second brand-owned regional source and as evidence of the split — **not** as a token source.

### Fonts

`tokens.typography.family` is **absent**. Microsoft YaHei is the Windows Simplified-Chinese
system face and Manrope is a webfont on a different surface; neither is a licensed Huawei
brand typeface established by this capture.

## Tier 2 cross-checks

getdesign.md and refero.design return no Huawei record — the ordinary CN outcome, and why the
regional rule requires two brand-owned sources instead. Both cited sources are Huawei-owned.

## Evidence limitations

- No component, and therefore no state, is claimed.
- Seven primitive hue ramps are published and unpainted on the measured surface; one step of
  each is recorded as evidence they exist, explicitly not as corporate-surface colours.
- No shadow, elevation, transition or duration token exists among the 339 properties.
- Desktop only. Enterprise, carrier and developer surfaces were not measured.
