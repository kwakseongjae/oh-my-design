  `accept-language`, light scheme forced, `getComputedStyle` on the document element and on
  individual controls; controls pinned by element handle, focus read for every control before

- Desktop only, light mode only. Captured 2026-09-22; authored 2026-09-23 from the saved
  capture with no values changed.

### Two collectors

A delegated subagent (Haiku) ran a first deep probe under round 3 of the collection-model trial
(`docs/COLLECTOR_MODEL_TRIAL_2026-09-22.md` §5). **Nothing from that run is used here.** It

states, missing the focus ring; called the AI CrowdWorks namespace possibly third-party; and
concluded webfonts were CORS-blocked. Every value below is from the independent re-measurement.

## Namespace ownership and the nonsense-path control

`https://crowdworks.jp/zz-this-does-not-exist` returns a real **HTTP 404** titled
「ページが見つかりませんでした【クラウドワークス】」 with a 664-character body. Not a catch-all. (It
loads the same stylesheet, so it reports the same 412 properties with different content.)

**412 properties; 17 are not CrowdWorks'.** `--fa-*` is Font Awesome 6 —
`--fa-font-sharp-light: normal 300 1em/1 "Font Awesome 6 Sharp"`, `--fa-font-brands`,
`--fa-font-light: … "Font Awesome 6 Pro"`, `--fa-font-sharp-solid`, and the rest of the set.
Excluded by name and by value.

**`--aicw-*` (56) is CrowdWorks' own**, not a third party: `--z-aicw-projects-login-gate-overlay`
places it inside the parent's z-index namespace, and it is AI CrowdWorks, the company's AI
product.

Namespaces of the remaining 395: `--z-*` 91 · `--aicw-*` 56 · `--space-*` 16 ·
`--paragraph-*` 16 · `--single-*` 16 · `--line-*` 15 · `--surface-*` 13 · `--text-*` 13 ·
`--font-*` 13 · eleven colour ramps of 10–11 each · `--shadow-*` 9 · `--border-*` 7 ·
`--background-*` 4 · `--object-*` 2 · `--blur-*` 1.
