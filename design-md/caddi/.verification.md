## Namespace ownership and the nonsense-path control

`https://caddi.com/ja-jp/zz-this-does-not-exist` returns a real **HTTP 404** titled
`Page not found | CADDi` with its own 1,149-character body. Not a catch-all.

All 158 custom properties carry CADDi's own naming across nine namespaces: `--space-*` 54 ·
`--label-*` 46 · `--color-*` 25 · `--font-*` 16 · `--line-*` 5 · `--radius-*` 4 · `--caddi-*` 3
· `--size-*` 3 · `--letter-*` 2. No framework prefix appears.

The page is built on Builder.io — one control carries `builder-block builder-510fc872f0b3`
classes — but the CMS contributes **no custom properties**, and the styling on that block is
CADDi's. It is flagged in §4 of the reference for a different reason: its focus ring is the
browser's, unlike the `CaddiButton` components'.

## Which surface the components came from

A first pass measured the dark `CaddiButton` and the language select on CADDi's **404 page**,
which would have been an odd thing to cite. Both were then re-measured on
`https://caddi.com/ja-jp/platform/`, a real content page, and that is what the claims point at.
The landing surface `caddi.com/ja-jp/` carries only two measurable controls; `/platform/` and
`/company/` carry the same set and the same 158 properties.

## The `--label-*` layer, recorded and not promoted

46 properties carry **interface copy**, not style: `--label-login` `"Log in"`,
`--label-drawer` `"CADDi Drawer"`, `--label-copyright` `"© CADDi, Inc."`,
`--label-privacy-recruiting` `"Recruiting Privacy Policy"`, `--label-auto` `"Automotive"`,
`--label-plant` `"Plant & Chemical"`, and so on — all in English on a `ja-jp` page.

Two defects are recorded as found:

- **`--label-mission` resolves to `"文字列値"`** — "string value". A placeholder in production.
- Six tokens have degenerate names: `--label---` `"Previous"`, `--label-----` `"Announcements"`,
  `--label------` `"Back to list"`, `--label-------` `"Media coverage"`,
  `--label--------` `"Press releases"`. A slug generator that ran out of input.

Neither is promoted into the token block. They are in the reference's §14 because a consumer of
this system should know the `--label-*` layer is generated and that nothing validates it.

## `--font-family-display` is declared and does not load

`"Zalando Sans Expanded"` appears in no font the browser reported loading on any of the three
measured pages. It is named in §3 as declared-and-unobserved and is **not** recorded in
`tokens.typography.family`, which carries the two faces that do load plus the Japanese face the
fallback chain reaches.

## Evidence limitations

- **Three controls**, all on public marketing surfaces. CADDi Drawer and CADDi Quote are behind
  authentication and were not opened, so the product UI is unmeasured.
- **The radius tokens were not observed in use.** They are recorded as published; §4 says the
  page does not draw on them.
- **No shadow token and none observed. No motion token** — the three components carry three
  different inline durations.
- The white `CaddiButton` is excluded because its hover could not be measured twice over.
- `18px` and `28px` render on the page and are not published type steps; recorded in the census
  above and not added to the scale.
- Desktop 1440×1100, light scheme, logged out.
