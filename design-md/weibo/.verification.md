# Weibo — Verification Notes (2026-09-22)

Pipeline: CN expansion wave 1, brand #1 · Brand: Weibo (微博 / Sina Weibo)

## Scope and collector boundary

- Measured with Playwright-driven Chrome, 1440×900, `zh-CN` locale and
  `accept-language: zh-CN`, light scheme, reading `getComputedStyle` on the document element
  and on individual controls. No stored collector bundle exists for Weibo.
- **The logged-out home is a sign-in wall over most of the product.** The feed chrome, token
  set and two controls are observable; the composer, super-topic surfaces, video player and
  every authenticated view are not. Nothing is claimed for them.
- Locale was forced to `zh-CN` deliberately. Measuring a Chinese brand through a default
  locale is how `mi.com` served a **Korean** page in this same wave's candidate probe.

### Fonts

`tokens.typography.family` is **absent, deliberately**. The body stack computes verbatim as
`QuoteFallback, system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Ubuntu, Cantarell, "Noto Sans", sans-serif`.
`QuoteFallback` is a **deliberately non-existent sentinel family**, so body text resolves to
the OS UI font. Weibo ships no brand typeface, and a system stack is never promoted to one.
pixiv uses the identical trick with `win-bug-omega`.

## Source-domain separation

| Domain | Source | Supports | Does not support |
|---|---|---|---|
| Live product | `weibo.com` logged-out home | Every colour, type and radius token; both measured controls and their states | Authenticated feed, composer, super-topics, video, mobile |
| Open platform | `open.weibo.com` | That Weibo operates the developer platform — brand ownership, second regional Tier-1 | **Any product token** — see below |

**`open.weibo.com` is deliberately not a token source.** It resolves only 26 custom properties,
sets `Inter` as its body face, and its call-to-action is `#fa8c16` darkening to `#e67e15` —
`#fa8c16` is **Ant Design's `orange-6`**, not Weibo's `#ff8200`. A generic developer portal on
a Weibo domain is still a generic developer portal. Importing its orange would have put a
component-library default into the brand's palette.

## Third-party properties present and excluded

`weibo.com` also resolves `--vc-*` (41), `--vs-*` (39) and a block of
`--color-prettylights-syntax-*`, `--fgColor-*` and `--borderColor-*`. The `prettylights` set is
**GitHub's syntax-highlighting theme**, arriving via an embedded stylesheet. None of these are
recorded. A frequency-based or namespace-blind extraction would have taken them as Weibo's.

## Tier 2 cross-checks

getdesign.md and refero.design return no Weibo record, the ordinary outcome for a CN brand and
the reason `REGIONAL_PROOF_COUNTRIES` requires two brand-owned regional Tier-1 sources instead.
Both cited sources are on Weibo's own domains.

## Evidence limitations

- **Two components only.** The sign-in wall leaves the feed card, composer, super-topic chrome
  and video player unobservable. They are not declared.
- No disabled state was observed on a rendered control, though `--w-disabled` exists as a token;
  no disabled component is declared.
- No shadow, elevation, transition or duration token appears among the 309 Weibo properties,
  and none was observed. §6 and §15 claim nothing.
- Desktop 1440×900 only. `m.weibo.cn` is a separate application and was not inspected.
- No persona, motion or responsive claim is made from a UI capture.
