## Namespace ownership and the nonsense-path control

The homepage has 806 computed root properties and 1,063 declared properties. Computed prefixes: `--krds-*` 796; `--color-*` 5; `--swiper-*` 2; and `--gap`, `--unit-height`, `--max-width` one each. Declared prefixes: KRDS 1,050; color 5; swiper 3; tooltip 2; max/gap/unit one each.

- KRDS is a shared government design-system namespace, not a corporation-specific brand namespace. Its declared-only palette is excluded. Seen values may be exported under an observed homepage role, without asserting corporate ownership of the framework.
- `--swiper-*` and the loaded `swiper-icons` face belong to carousel infrastructure. The stock `#007aff` colour is excluded. The observed active pagination fill is named by its local role, not as a brand primary.
- `--tooltip-arrow-*` is declared on `.krds-tooltip-popover::before`; it is classified with KRDS, not a separate brand namespace.
- The bare `--color-*`, `--max-width`, `--gap`, `--unit-height` group is present, but its authorship is unresolved. No declared-only colour is promoted from it. There are no Tailwind, Bootstrap, MUI, WordPress, Font Awesome or consent-manager property groups in the captured property inventory.
