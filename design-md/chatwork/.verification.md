# Chatwork — Verification Notes (2026-09-22)

Pipeline: JP expansion wave 1, brand #3 · Brand: Chatwork, operated by 株式会社kubell

## Scope and collector boundary

- Playwright-driven Chrome, 1440×1000, `ja-JP` with a matching `accept-language`, light scheme
  forced, `getComputedStyle` on the document element, on individual controls and — for the
  ownership question below — on the page's own CSS rules. Clicks suppressed in the capture
  phase, the page scrolled the full height and returned to the top.
- Logged out. No account was created and no sign-in was attempted. `www.chatwork.com`
  redirects to `/authentication.php`, the application's sign-in, and was **not** entered.
- Desktop only. The chat application itself was not measured.

## Namespace ownership and the nonsense-path control

`https://go.chatwork.com/ja/zz-this-does-not-exist` returns a real **HTTP 404** titled
`Chatwork | Page not found.` with its own 1,051-character body. Not a catch-all.

All 20 custom properties carry the `--rise--` prefix. RISE is the system's own name, and the
token names are Chatwork-specific (`--rise--color-cw-black`,
`--rise--fontFamily-chatworkSansB`), so no library-versus-brand question arises.

## The part that needed separating: an embedded Marketo form

The page embeds a Marketo form, and its styling is **not Chatwork's**. Three values were traced
to it and excluded:

- `#bf0000` — six `div.mktoAsterix` required-field markers.
- `rgb(60, 72, 14)` (`#3c480e`) — found in the stylesheet as
  `.mktoButton:hover { background: rgb(60, 72, 14) }` and `.mktoButton:focus { border-color: … }`.
- The one `box-shadow` on the page, `rgba(0,0,0,0.05) 0 3px 6px`, on `input.mktoField`.

A first pass measured `.mktoButton` as though it were a Chatwork CTA — 230×45, radius 26px,
16px/800, shrinking to 207×41 on hover. Every one of those values is Marketo's. Chatwork's own
primary is a different element entirely (below), and the two were separated by walking each
element's ancestors for a `mkto` class before counting it.

## The `#645b4a` question, and how it was settled

Hover on the red text actions returns `rgb(100, 91, 74)` — an olive-brown against a red brand,
which is exactly the shape of a mid-transition capture or an inherited default. Three checks:

1. **Reproduced on a fresh page per state** with a settle wait, through
   `probe-component-states.mjs`. Identical value.
2. **The transition cannot be mid-flight**: the controls declare `transition: all` with no
   duration, which resolves to `0s`.
3. **Found in the stylesheet, nine times**, including
   `.btn.btn--secondary { border: 1px solid rgb(100, 91, 74); background-color: rgb(100, 91, 74); }`
   and `.btn.plan__btn-signup--personal { border-color: rgb(100, 91, 74); background-color: rgb(100, 91, 74); }`.

It is Chatwork's secondary colour, authored, and it is recorded as `tokens.colors.secondary`.

## Second surface — kubell.com, and what it shows

`https://www.kubell.com/` (HTTP 200, `lang="ja"`, 2,817 characters) is the operating company's
own site. It states that Chatwork株式会社 became 株式会社kubell on **1 July 2024**, that it
operates Chatwork — 「日本最大級のビジネスチャット」 — used by **430,000 companies as of
December 2023**, alongside Taxita, Secure SAMBA and a cloud postal service.

It is cited for ownership and for those facts, **not for tokens**, and the reason is itself a
finding: kubell.com carries **no `--rise--*` at all**. It renders `Noto Sans JP` (169 elements)
and `Poppins` (27), an orange `#f04600` and a `#121212` ink. The parent company's visual system
and the product's are different systems on different type. The product kept its identity
through the rename.

## Evidence limitations

- **One marketing surface.** The chat application was not entered; `www.chatwork.com`
  redirects to its sign-in and was left alone.
- **Two of the four declared font weights were observed loading** (`R` and `B`). `Chatwork
  Sans L` and `EB` are declared in RISE and were not seen rendering on this page.
- **No spacing, shadow or motion token exists in RISE**, so none is recorded. The radius values
  in the reference are rendered readings, not tokens — `5px` is the page's dominant radius and
  RISE does not name it.
- **The primary pill has no focus reading** because it is a `<span>`; its hover and pressed
  were measured and are unchanged.
- The Marketo form's colours, button and shadow are excluded by ancestor class.
- Desktop 1440×1000, light scheme, logged out.
