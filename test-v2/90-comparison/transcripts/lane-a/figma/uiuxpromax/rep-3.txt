Heading: Motion

Transforms and colors only. 180ms ease. `prefers-reduced-motion` zeros duration. html-tailwind: no bounce.

Heading: Offline / integrity

- No `@import`, no Google Fonts, no CDN, no remote images.
- Reset sets `margin: 0; padding: 0` on `figure`, `blockquote`, `ul`, `ol` (UA `figure { margin: 1em 40px }` overflows a 100% hero).
- `img` has width/height and `max-width: 100%`.
- Body font stack is sans-serif (not Times).
