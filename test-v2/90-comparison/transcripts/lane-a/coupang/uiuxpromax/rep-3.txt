Heading: Pattern

**Marketplace / Directory** (landing + design-system hit).

- Search bar is the CTA. Reduce friction to search.
- Placement: sticky header search (always reachable) + home hero still in view on first paint.
- Sections used: header search, category entry, featured campaign, curated listings (`오늘의 발견`), then product list after submit.
- Not used: seller-host CTA page, trust/safety landing, extra routes. Brief is home + search destination only.
