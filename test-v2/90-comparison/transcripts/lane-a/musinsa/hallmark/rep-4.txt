Heading: MUSINSA 상의 — Sport / Catalogue

Isolated run. No project , no cached pre-flight, first stamp in this directory.

Going with: audience = Korean fashion shoppers on a category listing · use = apply one color filter and scan the remaining tops · tone = utilitarian.

Heading: Picks

- Genre: editorial (silent default; commerce listing, not SaaS / atmospheric / playful)
- Macrostructure: Catalogue
- Theme: Sport — light paper, condensed roman display, warm signal accent
- Nav: N12 announcement banner + retracting bar (commerce default; editorial-acceptable). Knobs: fill=solid, dismiss=yes, bar=sticky
- Footer: Ft2 single credit line. Knobs: wordmark · credit, middot, spaced
- Feature: F6 product card grid. Knobs: 3/4 portrait, 5-up at 60rem, Save
- Enrichment: no typographic hero. Photographic specimens from the 2026-08-22 evidence capture, plus one generated still (`image.jpg`) for the black rolled-sleeve tee
- Motion: Sport 0.7×. Banner retract, chip press, save toggle. No scroll-fade grid

Previous nav: none. This build: N12, because the brief is a shop floor with a time-bound 타임세일 strip already in the capture.

No network fonts. Hangul rides the body stack. Two families. Spacing is the 4pt named scale. Radius 2–4px. Hairline rules, no card shadow.

Heading: Screen

One screen: 상의 category. Task state on first paint: **컬러 블랙** is pressed. The grid shows three black tops. White and charcoal SKUs stay in the DOM and appear only when those swatches are pressed. Ivory and grey have no matching SKUs; the empty line is honest.

Counts are the visible card count, not the capture’s unfiltered 350,441. Prices, sale percents, like counts, and gender marks are copied from the capture where they exist. Missing fields are `—`.

Wordmark is the Latin string `MUSINSA`. No official mark file.

Heading: Components

- N12 banner: “타임세일 · 상의”. Dismiss zeroes `--banner-h`. Scroll-down retracts the banner.
- Colour chips: eight states (default, hover, focus-visible, active, disabled, loading, error, success/pressed).
- F6 cards: image, brand, name, price, save. Uniform 3/4. Grid tracks `minmax(0, 1fr)`. 2-up <40rem, 3-up ≥40rem, 5-up ≥60rem.
- Ft2: one line, nowrap from 40rem.

Heading: States

Interactive controls implement default · hover · `:focus-visible` · `:active` · disabled · loading · error · success. Focus rings are instant, 2px, `--color-focus`. Hit targets 44px. `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` collapses spatial motion to 150ms.

Save is optimistic and silent. No toast.

Heading: Offline

`render.html` loads only sibling files (`image.jpg`, `p-*.png`). CSS reset zeroes `figure`, `blockquote`, `ul`, `ol`. `html`/`body` use `overflow-x: clip`.
