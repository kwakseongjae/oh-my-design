Heading: Style guidelines

**Applied pattern:** Hero-Centric Design (landing search), not pack Minimal Single Column.

**Pack style name (database):** Exaggerated Minimalism — keep oversized type and whitespace; drop the dark canvas and 900-weight clamp the database attached.

**Key effects used:** large hero type (80px / 40px), whitespace around the still, 150–200ms chrome transitions.

**Key effects not used:** `font-weight: 900`, `clamp(3rem 10vw 12rem)` — those are pack, not evidence.


Heading: Anti-patterns (Do NOT Use)

- Pack: playful design; unclear fees; AI purple/pink gradients
- Pack: emojis as icons; missing `cursor: pointer`; layout-shifting hover; contrast below 4.5:1 for body; instant state changes; invisible focus
- This run: extra screens not in the brief; hexes not in evidence.json or the verify capture; Google Fonts / CDN; treating pack Fintech/Crypto gold as Toss; inventing a service mega-menu
- Evidence `method.note`: do not collapse imagery to “the one hero photo” as if the funnel were complete (`desktop-1440.imagery.attrition.suspect: true`, only 2 samples)
