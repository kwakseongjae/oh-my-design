Heading: Stance

One job on this home: move from the observed top nav into 서비스. Density, extra marketing chapters, and unverified product tiles are sacrificed so the first paint can stay snapshot-bound.

Heading: Principles (each names a sacrifice)

1. **Snapshot chrome over brand memory.** Only labels, type sizes, and colours that the capture records or that a pixel sample of the viewport confirms. Sacrifice: no mega-menu of products remembered from outside this snapshot.
2. **One primary action.** `앱 다운로드` is the only `data-cta="primary"`. 서비스 is navigation, not a second hero button. Sacrifice: no sticky duplicate CTA.
3. **Hangul first, unloadable faces omitted.** Evidence records `Toss Product Sans OTF`; this run cannot load it (no network, no font file). Display hierarchy uses weight and the pack’s Korean gothic stack. Sacrifice: no fake webfont that pretends to be Toss Product Sans.

Heading: Layout & Platforms
