Heading: Pattern

- **Name:** Minimal Single Column
- **Conversion Focus:** Single CTA focus. Large typography. Lots of whitespace. Mobile-first.
- **CTA Placement:** Center, large CTA button
- **Color Strategy (pack):** Minimalist: Brand + white + accent. Buttons: High contrast 7:1+. Text: Black/Dark grey
- **Sections (pack):** 1. Hero headline, 2. Short description, 3. Benefit bullets (3 max), 4. CTA, 5. Footer

**Screen binding:** the specified screen is Home (`https://www.apple.com/kr/`). Evidence first viewport is a product-hero column (headline + short line + two CTAs + family photograph), then the next product band. Benefit bullets and footer are **absent** in evidence and are not invented.

Landing-domain search (`hero product cta photography`) returned Product Demo + Features first. The live surface keeps the pack Minimal Single Column + evidence hero, not a demo video (motion is false).


Heading: Style

- **Name:** Exaggerated Minimalism
- **Mode Support:** Light ✓ Full | Dark ✓ Full
- **Keywords:** Bold minimalism, oversized typography, high contrast, negative space
- **Best For:** Fashion, architecture, portfolios, agency landing pages, luxury brands, editorial
- **Performance:** Excellent | **Accessibility:** WCAG AA

Style-domain search also ranked **Minimalism & Swiss Style** (monochrome, sans-serif, grid, no unnecessary decoration). The live surface follows that checklist more closely than fashion-editorial serif, because evidence type is a sans family and the photograph is the statement.

Style search results not used: Vintage Analog, Voice-First Multimodal, Zero Interface.

Heading: UX rules applied (pack Quick Reference + domain search)

- Skip link to `#main`
- Sequential headings (`h1` hero, `h2` next band)
- Visible focus rings; do not remove outline without replacement
- Keyboard tab order matches visual order
- Touch targets ≥ 44×44px; ≥ 8px gap
- `cursor: pointer` on clickable elements
- `touch-action: manipulation`
- `prefers-reduced-motion`
- z-index scale 40 / 50 / 100 (not 9999)
- Hero image eager; width/height via aspect-ratio
- No hover-only primary action
