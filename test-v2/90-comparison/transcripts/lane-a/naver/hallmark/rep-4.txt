Heading: Page shape

1. **N12** — cream announcement, dismiss ×, retracts on scroll-down.
2. **Bar** — menu, pay (text, not a logo), C2 search pill, notify, cart.
3. **Shortcut rail** — ten destinations, type marks.
4. **Promo** — capture headline + `image.jpg` (LCP, `fetchpriority="high"`).
5. **F3 newsstand** — tab row (S4) + ticker + publisher name grid (text, not press logos).
6. **Login** — filled accent button.
7. **Ad slot** — dates from the capture, labelled 광고. No third-party mark.
8. **Shopping chrome** — tab labels from the capture; no invented SKUs or prices.
9. **Ft2** — wordmark · 홈 · snapshot date.
10. **Results view** — same HTML file, same search. No extra product screens.

Search is the only task path. Empty submit sets an error on the field (what / what to do). A non-empty submit writes `?q=` and swaps `data-view="results"`.

Heading: States (search, login, icon buttons)

Default · hover (`hover: hover`) · `:focus-visible` (instant ring, no fade) · `:active` (`translateY(1px)`) · `:disabled` (opacity + cursor + attribute) · loading (`is-loading` / `data-state`) · error (`is-error` + `aria-invalid` + helper) · success (`is-success` after a query lands).

Input border-width stays 1px. Helper slot is `min-height: 1lh` and absolutely placed so it does not shove the shortcut rail.

Heading: Motion

Editorial default-off. Two primitives only: N12 banner translate, control press `translateY`. `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` collapses durations to 150ms. No `transition-all`, no hover-scale, no scroll-fade.

Heading: Copy

Capture copy only: banner, placeholder, shortcuts, promo, newsstand names, ticker, login, ad dates, whale dialog, shopping tabs. No invented metrics. Results rows echo the user’s query; they do not fabricate article titles or hit counts.
