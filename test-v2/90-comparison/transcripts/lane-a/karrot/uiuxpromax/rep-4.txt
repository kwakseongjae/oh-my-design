Heading: Domain supplements (Step 3)

- **product** `marketplace listing cards search results` → Classifieds / Buy-Sell. Style: **Flat Design + Vibrant & Block-based**. Landing: Filter-Heavy Grid. Palette: neutral bg + price emphasis + category chips.
- **style** `content-first marketplace minimal` → Flat Design Mobile (touch-first): zero elevation, 8px radius, press scale 0.97, 4/8 spacing, 48px targets.
- **color** `marketplace orange local commerce` → Local Events orange (same hex table as `--design-system`).
- **typography** `modern sans readable` → **Korean Modern**: Noto Sans KR / Noto Sans KR. Cannot fetch Google Fonts offline → system Korean stack.
- **ux** search/listing: predictable **back** (`hash` + history, not `location.replace`); heading order; keyboard; skip link.
- **icons** Phosphor outline: `magnifying-glass`, `funnel`, `map-pin`/`gps`, `sidebar`/menu. Inlined SVG (no `@phosphor-icons/react`).
- **stack html-tailwind** `card grid search results list` → consistent card structure; `grid` + `gap` (not per-item margins).

Product-domain **Flat listing grid** wins over the density-biased “dashboard” skin. Density 8 is kept as **4-column / 2-column card packing** and 8–32px spacing, not KPI widgets.

Heading: Motion

Dial 2 + evidence `motion.present: false` → no GSAP, no scroll reveal. Only 160ms color/transform on press/hover/focus. `prefers-reduced-motion` zeroes transitions and press scale.

Heading: Accessibility (pack §1–3)

- Contrast: ink `#1A1C20` on white ≫ 4.5:1; muted `#64748B` on white ≥ 3:1 for secondary.
- Focus visible 2+2px ring (`--color-ring`).
- Icon-only controls have `aria-label`.
- Heading order: results `h1` → `h2` 필터 / `h2` 매물 목록 → card `h2`; detail `h1` = listing title.
- Skip link. Tab order follows visual order. Cards are real links.
- Touch ≥ 44px. `touch-action: manipulation`.
- `aria-live` announces result vs detail.
- Viewport zoom not disabled.
