Heading: Surfaces

One HTML file, two views, no extra screens:

1. Search results — inventory header `“노트북” 검색 결과`, location, filters, 8 listing cards from the snapshot.
2. Listing detail — opened from a card (or from ⌘K). Image, title, price, neighbourhood, time. Back is `검색 결과로`.

Wordmark is the text `당근`. No official mark.

Heading: Type

- Display / body: Plus Jakarta Sans 400–700, embedded as woff2 (offline). Hangul glyphs fall through to `Apple SD Gothic Neo` / `Noto Sans KR` / `Malgun Gothic`.
- Outlier (≤ 2 slots): JetBrains Mono on the 연관 검색어 label and the command-palette group label.
- Scale: major third from 16px. Inventory title uses `--text-display-s` (Catalogue has no marquee display).
- Headings roman. Tabular numerals on prices.

Heading: Colour (OKLCH, named tokens only)

Cream paper `oklch(97% 0.012 95)`, cool ink `oklch(20% 0.012 250)`, pear accent, cyan secondary, coral pop. No pure white paper, no pure black ink. Accent-ink is the same cool ink (pear is light). Focus is cyan at `oklch(48% 0.16 235)`.

Heading: Space & layout

4pt scale `--space-3xs`…`--space-3xl`. Left-biased: filter rail + catalogue on desktop; stacked on small screens. Image tracks use `minmax(0, 1fr)`. Root `overflow-x: clip`. Card radius 20px, pills 999px, inputs 12px.

Heading: Interaction

Hum `.btn` press (edge shadow, lift on hover, press down on active). Cards: colour-shift tint 6% → 12% plus 4px lift. Command palette: ⌘K / pill, Esc, arrows, Enter. Eight states coded on `.btn` and `.searchpill` (default, hover, focus, active, disabled, loading, error, success). Focus rings are instant `:focus-visible`. `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` collapses spatial motion.

Heading: Copy

Listing titles, prices, neighbourhood, bump times, related terms, and filter labels come from snapshot `2026-08-22T19-49-29-044Z`. No invented counts, testimonials, or seller copy.

`image.jpg` is the representative listing photo for the first card and its detail. Other cards use CSS laptop placeholders so the file stays offline.
