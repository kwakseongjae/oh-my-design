Heading: Tokens

All colours are OKLCH custom properties. Paper is warm near-white, not `#fff`. Ink is cool near-black, not `#000`. Accent is the observed orange, used as the wordmark (~3% of the viewport).

Heading: Layout

Desktop (≥ 60rem): sticky header (wordmark · search pill · service chips) → related terms → inventory title → filter rail + 4-up square cards.

Mobile: wordmark · search · menu. Location / filter chips above a 2-up grid. Rail hides. Hit targets 44px. `minmax(0, 1fr)` on image tracks. `overflow-x: clip` on `html` and `body`. Display titles `overflow-wrap: anywhere`.

Heading: Interaction

Cards are hash links (`#item-n`). `:target` swaps results for that listing’s photo, title, price, and neighborhood/time. Back is `#results`. No extra product screens.

Search pill opens a native `<dialog>` (also ⌘K). Choosing a related term stays on this page and rewrites the title. Menu is a disclosure of the same service list.

Eight states are declared on `.btn`, `.searchpill`, `.product`, and `.input` (default, hover, focus-visible, active, disabled, loading, error, success). Focus rings are instant. `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` collapses spatial motion to ≤150ms and kills the wordmark pulse and click burst.

Heading: Copy

Titles, prices, neighborhood, and bump times come from the capture. No invented counts, testimonials, or seller fields. Official mark is not used; the wordmark is the text “당근” plus a CSS disc.

Heading: Image

`image.jpg` is the first listing thumb and that listing’s detail photograph. Other thumbs are colour swatches sampled from the evidence palettes, not generated stock.
